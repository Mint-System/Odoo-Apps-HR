Testing HR Timesheet Attendance Recording
=========================================

1. Create a timesheet entry for 1 hour.
2. Check that an attendance record is created with check-in at 08:00.
3. Check the duration of the attendance created.
4. Create a second timesheet entry for 2 hours on the same date.
5. Verify the attendance check-out time is updated to 11:00.
6. Unlink one of the timesheet entries.
7. Verify the attendance still exists with the remaining timesheet.
8. Unlink the last timesheet entry.
9. Verify the attendance record is removed.
10. Check the smart-button on the attendance form shows the correct timesheet count.
