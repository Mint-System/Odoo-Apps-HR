overtime balances.

Usage
=====

1. Open *Attendances > Configuration > Overtime Rulesets* and open the
   *Extra Hours and Overtime* ruleset.
2. On the overtime rule set *Give back as time off* and select the *Time
   Off Type* the hours of this rule are credited to.
3. Assign the ruleset to the employee contract.
4. The balances per time off type are shown in the time off dashboard
   and in the time off request.
5. The report *Attendances > Reporting > Extra Hours and Overtime* shows
   the overtime lines grouped by time off type.
6. The attendance shows the extra hours in *Worked Extra Hours* and
   *Validated Extra Hours*. The hours of a rule with a weekly period,
   e.g. the hours above the 45 hour limit, are shown in *Weekly Overtime
   Hours* on the last attendance of the week.
